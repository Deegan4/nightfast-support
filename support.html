Frequently Asked Questions

🚀 Getting Started

How do I sign in to NightFast?

▼

NightFast uses Sign in with Apple for secure, private authentication. Tap "Sign in with Apple" and follow the prompts.

How do I enable Apple Health integration?

▼

Go to Settings > Health in the app and enable HealthKit sync. You'll be prompted to grant permissions for reading sleep data and writing nutrition data.

💳 Subscriptions & Billing

How do I cancel my subscription?

▼

Open Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > NightFast > Cancel Subscription. You'll have access until the end of your billing period.

How do I restore my purchases?

▼

Go to Settings > Subscription > Restore Purchases in the app. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID.

☁️ Sync & Data

How do I delete my account and data?

▼

Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data within 30 days and cannot be undone.

Troubleshooting

App Crashes or Won't Open

  1. Force quit the app by swiping up from the app switcher
  2. Restart your iPhone
  3. Check for app updates in the App Store
  4. Ensure you have enough storage space
  5. Try deleting and reinstalling (your data is safe in iCloud)

HealthKit Not Syncing

  1. Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Health
  2. Find NightFast and ensure all permissions are enabled
  3. In NightFast, go to Settings > Health and toggle sync off and on
