Apple Health Integration

NightFast integrates with Apple HealthKit to provide comprehensive health insights

📥 Data We Read (with your permission)

  • Sleep analysis data
  • Sleep duration and quality
  • Sleep schedule patterns

📤 Data We Write (with your permission)

  • Dietary energy (calories)
  • Protein intake
  • Carbohydrate intake
  • Fat intake
  • Caffeine consumption

🔒 Your Privacy

  • All Health data stays on your device
  • We never access your Health data on our servers
  • You control all permissions
  • Revoke access anytime in Settings

HealthKit integration is optional. NightFast works fully without Health access. You can enable or disable Health features at any time in the app's Settings.
